# Project Charter

## Identity
**DEALZ V2** — AI-powered Travel Planning & Booking Ecosystem. New product implementation sharing DealZ brand direction, not a continuation of V1.

## Vision
Help travelers move from an initial idea to a feasible, budget-aware trip and bookable services. Combine AI planning, verified destination data, interactive maps, budget planning, saved itineraries and a coherent multi-service booking experience.

## Core value
1. Generate editable day-by-day itineraries from natural language and structured preferences.
2. Recommend destinations and places based on dates, interests, group size and budget.
3. Visualize stops and route estimates on an interactive map.
4. Calculate transparent trip budgets, per-person estimates and remaining budget.
5. Match plans with accommodation, transport, outfit rental, photography, activities and tours.
6. Save, revise, duplicate and share trips.
7. Book/manage services with explicit supplier, price, terms and status.

## Users
Primary: Vietnamese domestic solo travelers, couples, friend groups and families. Later: international visitors with English UX and suitable payment methods.

## Product principles
Useful before monetized; truthful data; user remains in control; transparent budget; operationally feasible schedules; modular service bookings; privacy/security by design; mobile-first.

## Brand
Reference palette: Orange `#FD550A`, Deep Green `#015D47`, Lime `#E0F354`. Style: bold editorial typography, strong color blocking, energetic travel imagery. Keep planner, maps, finance and checkout calm and readable; do not use bright colors on every panel.

## Proposed revenue
Commission on completed supplier bookings; affiliate referrals where permitted; clearly labelled sponsored placement; premium planning features; supplier tools. Deposits/refunds/unpaid/cancelled orders are not earned revenue.

## Scope
MVP includes AI trip planning, itinerary editing, maps/routing, budget/expenses, service catalogue, supplier profiles, booking lifecycle, payment adapter, customer dashboard, partner portal foundation, admin operations, analytics and tests.

Not MVP by default: building flight/hotel inventory aggregation from scratch; guaranteed real-time availability without provider APIs; automatic third-party purchases without user approval; lending/credit scoring; complex multi-currency settlement before VND flow works.

## V1 boundary
No V1 code, schema, theme, plugin or snippet reused by default. Legacy ideas are candidates only and require review.
